## Research projects

- **[Federated Traffic Forecasting](https://github.com/marrkann08-max/100-days-ai-ml/tree/main/projects/008-fedtraffic)** — Can city districts train one traffic-forecasting model without sharing their sensor data? Graph and recurrent networks written from scratch in PyTorch, trained with Flower on METR-LA (207 sensors). Includes encoder benchmarks, communication trade-offs, differential privacy, and a real multi-process Flower deployment. Personalised federated learning beats every district training alone and comes within 0.5% of pooling all the data, with 95% confidence intervals over test days. [Report](https://github.com/marrkann08-max/100-days-ai-ml/blob/main/projects/008-fedtraffic/paper/report.md)
- **[Software Effort Estimator](https://github.com/marrkann08-max/software-effort-estimator)** — Research prototype building on Sehra et al. (2019): an FAHP-weighted SVR evaluated with nested cross-validation, compared with standard COCOMO and regression baselines, and tested on the external NASA93 dataset, where it generalises best. Includes a leakage-safe SHAP study of whether the weights match what the model relies on, and a dataset of 91 GitHub repositories.
